# Mission

Challenge the first design direction rather than agreeing with it. Propose a materially different composition, typographic architecture, signature device and responsive strategy while preserving the same factual brief and conversion objective.

# Rules

- Do not produce a recolor or superficial variant.
- Explain what visual assumption you are changing.
- Avoid novelty that damages readability, accessibility or conversion.
- Do not invent business evidence.
- Keep the proposal implementable in local HTML/CSS/JS.
